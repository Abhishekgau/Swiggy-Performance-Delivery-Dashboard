# 🛵 Swiggy Performance & Delivery Dashboard

An end-to-end data analytics dashboard built to monitor, analyze, and visualize food delivery performance, sales trends, restaurant metrics, and regional distributions using advanced Excel techniques.

---

## 📊 Project Overview
The **Swiggy Performance & Delivery Dashboard** provides deep insights into key business performance indicators (KPIs) for food delivery operations. It handles large-scale transactional datasets (`~18.3 MB`) to break down revenue streams, customer ratings, order volumes, and geographical sales distribution.

---

## 🚀 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Sales:** ₹53.01 M
* **Average Rating:** 4.34 / 5.0
* **Average Order Value:** ₹268.5 M
* **Total Rating Count:** 5.59 M
* **Total Orders:** 197.4 K

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features & Insights

1. **Filter Panel:** 
   * Interactive filters for **Food Type** (Veg / Non-Veg) and **Weeks** (Sun–Sat) to dynamically slice and dice performance metrics.
2. **Monthly Trend:** 
   * Visualizes sales fluctuations across months (January to August), highlighting peak revenue periods.
3. **Food Type Sales Distribution:** 
   * Doughnut chart breaking down sales share between Veg (67.23%) and Non-Veg (32.77%) items.
4. **Top 5 Restaurant Sales:** 
   * Horizontal bar chart highlighting top-performing partner restaurants (e.g., KFC, McDonald's, Pizza Hut, Burger King, Domino's Pizza).
5. **Weekly Trends:** 
   * Day-by-day delivery and sales volume distribution across the week.
6. **Quarterly Breakdown:** 
   * Tabular summary tracking Price, Rating, and Order volume across Quarters (Q1, Q2, Q3).
7. **State By Sales Map:** 
   * Geographical map visualization mapping sales volume distributions across different regions.
